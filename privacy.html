This policy explains what information the Liman Kaptanı mobile game ("the App") handles and why. The App is developed by an independent developer ("we", "us").

1. Information we collect

We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, email address, phone number or contacts. The App has no servers of its own and we do not receive your personal data.

Your game progress (unlocked levels, power-up counts, settings such as sound, vibration and language) is stored only on your device. It is deleted when you uninstall the App.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App is free and shows ads through Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. To serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect and process:

  • Device identifiers, including Apple's Advertising Identifier (IDFA), only if you allow tracking in the App Tracking Transparency prompt
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it
  • Device and app information (model, operating system, language, app version)
  • Ad interactions (ads shown, taps, rewarded ads watched)
  • Diagnostic and performance data related to ads

If you do not allow tracking, you will still see ads, but they will be less personalized. You can change this at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Users in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland are shown a consent form (Google User Messaging Platform) before personalized ads are used, and can change their choice later from the App's settings via "Privacy options".

To learn how Google uses this data, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services and the Google Privacy Policy.

3. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided data through the App, contact us and we will help remove it.

4. Data retention and your rights

We do not store personal data ourselves. Local game data stays on your device until you delete the App or use "Reset progress" in settings. For data processed by Google, you can use Google's tools or contact Google directly. Depending on where you live (for example under GDPR or KVKK), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your data and to object to processing.

5. Security

The App does not transmit your game data to us. Advertising traffic is handled by Google over encrypted connections.

6. Changes

We may update this policy. Changes will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.

7. Contact

Questions about privacy? Please contact us through the Support page.
