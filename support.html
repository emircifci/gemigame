FAQ

How do I play?

Tap a ship whose path to the pier is clear. It sails to a free berth and passengers of the same color board it. When it is full it leaves. If all berths fill up with ships nobody can board, the level is lost.

A ship won't move when I tap it.

Another ship is blocking its way. Free the ships in front first, or use the Tug power-up to pull it out.

What are fog and tide?

In fog, a ship's color stays hidden until its path is clear; the Lighthouse lifts the fog. With tide, sandbars appear at low tide and block some routes until the water rises again.

How do I get more power-ups?

Tap a power-up with no charges left and watch a short ad to get one for free.

How do I reset my progress?

Settings → Reset progress. This cannot be undone.

How do I change ad personalization?

iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. In the EEA/UK you can also use Settings → Privacy options in the game. See our Privacy Policy.
